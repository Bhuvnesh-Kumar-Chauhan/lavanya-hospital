How do I request an appointment?+

Use the enquiry form on this page or contact the hospital directly. The team can confirm available timings and next steps.

Can I check which doctors are available?+

Doctor profiles and schedules will be updated after verification by the hospital. Please call the hospital to confirm current availability.

Where is Lavanya Hospital located?+

Lavanya Hospital is in Kashipur, Uttarakhand. The exact address and map link should be added to the Contact section before launch.

Does submitting this form confirm an appointment?+

No. This form is for an enquiry only. An appointment is confirmed only after the hospital team contacts you and confirms the details.

What should I do in a medical emergency?+

Do not wait for a website response. Contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.
